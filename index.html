RNA Club Warsaw

A monthly meeting for everyone working on RNA on the Ochota Campus: talks with unpublished data, open discussion, then pizza and time to talk with the labs next door.

Monthly, Wednesday 15:00 tbc 4 host institutes: CeNT UW, IIMCB, IBB PAS, IMol PAS Not streamed, not recorded

About

Ten years of RNA talk on campus

RNA Club Warsaw started in June 2016 with a kick-off meeting at IIMCB, organised by Marcin Magnus as a spin-off of Do Science! with support from prof. Jacek Kuźnicki. From October 2016 it was funded as an RNA Salon by the RNA Society, later with Lexogen as co-sponsor. The aim has stayed the same: help labs working on RNA learn what their neighbours do, share methods and reagents, and start collaborations.

The club has hosted close to fifty speakers so far. In autumn 2026 it restarts as a regular monthly series, rotating between the four institutes of the Ochota Campus, and celebrates its tenth anniversary this season.

Who it is for

PhD students, postdocs, PIs

Anyone on campus whose work touches RNA: transcription, processing and decay, translation, modifications, structure, RNA viruses, mRNA therapeutics, chemistry and computation. Members of other Warsaw institutes are welcome too.

Talks are deliberately kept off the record so speakers can show unpublished data and early projects and get real feedback.

Meeting format

One afternoon a month

  1. 15:00
    Talk 1 · 20 min + 10 min discussionEarly enough for people who collect children after work.
  2. 15:30
    Talk 2 · 20 min + 10 min discussionEach lab gets one slot. It can be split between two people.
  3. 16:00
    Pizza and drinksInformal networking at the host institute, paid from the club budget.
  4. later
    OptionalContinue the discussion over a beer nearby.

Ground rules

  • Speakers come from labs outside the hosting institute.
  • No streaming, no recording. Unpublished work is welcome.
  • Each lab decides who speaks and what they present.
  • Before each meeting we ask for a quick RSVP so we order the right amount of food.

Planned extras

  • A yearly meeting with RNA biotech companies.
  • A spring session, for example with those heading to the RNA Society meeting in Strasbourg.
  • A booklet of all RNA labs on campus, made from the kick-off flash talks.

Kick-off and schedule

First meeting room booked

Wednesday, 14 October 2026 · 15:00

CeNT UW, Centre of New Technologies, University of Warsaw

Lab flash talks. Every PI introduces their lab in about 3 minutes and 1–3 slides, all in one shared slide deck. Two rounds with a coffee break in between, then pizza and networking. At the event we will also fill in the talk schedule for the season.

Suggested content for your slides

  • Lab name, institute, and your main research question in one or two sentences
  • Model systems and methods
  • What you can offer: techniques, cell lines, equipment, expertise
  • What you are looking for: collaborators, methods, materials
